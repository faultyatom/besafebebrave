About

I'm Shivani S (they/them), a digital security consultant. I've spent the last seven years helping activists, journalists, lawyers and grassroots organisations in India protect themselves and the people who trust them — first by building a digital security practice inside a national rights organisation, and since 2022 independently.

Most of my work happens in rooms full of people who have been told technology is beyond them. It isn't. The job is to make the risks legible and the responses practical, in the language people think in.
