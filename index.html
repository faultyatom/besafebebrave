Your photo is talking about you.

Every photo your phone takes carries a second, invisible file: where you were, what you were holding, and the exact second you pressed the button. Most people have never seen it. Drop in a photo and look at yours.

This runs entirely inside your browser. Your photo is never uploaded, never stored, and never sent anywhere — there is no server to send it to. If you'd rather not take that on trust, open your browser's network tab and watch while you drop the file in. Nothing will happen, which is the point.
